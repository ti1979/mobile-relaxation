Mobile Relaxation is a small wellness and educational project operated by **Amblery**.

Our goal is to provide simple, accessible information about breathing exercises, relaxation practices, mindfulness, and related wellness topics.

The site introduces a variety of breathing and relaxation techniques in an easy-to-understand format. Where appropriate, we also provide links to external reference materials, including Wikipedia and other publicly available resources, so that readers can explore the background and history of individual practices in greater detail.

## Our Approach

We aim to present wellness practices as information and opportunities for personal exploration rather than as guaranteed medical treatments.

Some of the practices discussed on this website have been studied scientifically, while others have limited, mixed, or inconclusive evidence. We therefore encourage readers to consider the available evidence and to consult qualified healthcare professionals when they have questions about their individual health.

The information on this website is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
